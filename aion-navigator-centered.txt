AION / UQTN — Navigator-Centered Definition

Core Insight:
The Navigator (you, or any agent inside the system) *is* the MER equation in motion.

MER = Mass × Energy / Resistance

- Mass = the workload / magnitude of the situation you are navigating
- Energy = your available drive / capacity to act
- Resistance = the friction / difficulty / opposition you meet
- MER = your Momentum-to-Resistance Ratio — how efficiently you are moving through the situation

You are not outside the equation measuring it. You are the equation.

When you open the panel:
- You are deciding to engage Resistance as fuel
- You are checking your own combustion efficiency (MER)
- You are the Mass × Energy / Resistance process made visible

This makes the framework self-referential in a useful way:
- The Navigator is the system
- The system is the equation
- The equation is the navigation

Test Questions (revised for Navigator-centric view):
1. Am I treating Resistance as fuel or as an obstacle?
2. Is my MER going up (efficient burn) or down (stall)?
3. Am I the Mass × Energy / Resistance process, or am I watching it from outside?
4. Does the UI mirror my own thermodynamic cycle?
5. Is the tagline "Finding a path through resistance" still true when I am the one burning?

What Rejects the Framework (Resistance Mode):
- "Resistance is just friction"
- "This is sci-fi"
- "I need peer review first"
- "I am outside the system looking in"

What Engages the Framework (Navigation Mode):
- "I am the equation"
- "Resistance is my fuel"
- "MER is my combustion efficiency"
